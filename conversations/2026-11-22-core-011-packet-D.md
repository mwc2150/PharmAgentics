## Conventions

- Per-week averages: a "per week" window is the sum over the weeks that have rows divided by the number of distinct weeks, so 4-week and 5-week windows compare fairly. Used for every window-to-window change.
- Windows: "summer" = 2025-06-02 to 2025-08-01 (9 weeks); "fall" = 2025-08-04 to 2025-10-27 (13 weeks); "Jan" = 2026-01-05 to 01-26; "Great Lakes gap" = 2026-02-02 to 02-23; "Southeast gap" = 2026-08-03 to 08-31 against July 2026-07-06 to 07-27.
- Role: `must` for 2 to 4 numbers per case that the story hinges on; everything else `context`. Existing facts were tagged with `role` only; their SQL and values are unchanged.
