# brief-010 packet N: the national competitor card (2026-11-22)

Branch `feature/brief-010-national`. Spec: brief-010 FR-006, User Story 4; core-011 FR-004, FR-017.

## What changed
- `tools/checks.py`: `check_competitor_national(reg, W)` (new). Supported once Metacora has launched and holds at least `COMPETITOR_FLOOR` (5 points) of the basket on the brief's weeks; ruled out before launch or below the floor. Its text carries no number, so it adds no claim.
- `tools/stages.py`: `competitor_status` now also fills `facts` through `_national_reading`: the check, `directions` (`bxa_share`, `competitor_share`, `trx`; share points and TRx change both use `stages.word`, flat inside 0.5). No comparison weeks: no directions. TRx is like-for-like (`p.ok`).
- `tools/memo.py`: `competitor_card` builds the national card. Shape: `territory: "NATIONAL"`, `cut: None`, `direction` = BXA basket-share direction, `directions` {bxa_share, competitor_share, trx}, `drivers_checked: ["competitor"]`, `drivers_supported` / `drivers_ruled_out` from the check, confidence Medium. Headline unchanged ("Metacora holds {bxe} of the four-brand basket, and BXA holds {bxa}."). Both engines already call `competitor_status` then `competitor_card`, so the agent engine gets the same card; there is one competitor block, so at most one national card a brief.
- `eval/harness/scorers.py`: `_finding_points` reads `card["directions"][measure]` when the finding has a `measure`, else `card["direction"]`.
- `CARD_FIELDS` (leader allow-list) is unchanged, so `directions` stays analyst-only; leaders see the card with territory NATIONAL.

## Follow-ups from the integrator (measure coverage and national ruled-out)
- (a) A card covers the measures in its `directions`; a card with no `directions` covers `trx` only; a finding with no `measure` is `trx`. `_finding_points` and `false_drivers` now consider only cards that cover the finding's measure (`_measure`, `_covers`, `_seen` in `eval/harness/scorers.py`). `trx` is removed from the national card's `directions` (it is a share story; national TRx is in the KPI strip), so the card holds `{bxa_share, competitor_share}`. Result: a national competitor card is no false driver and earns no point for TRx findings like P2-Q1, P3-Q2 and WIN-1 (tested). This supersedes the false-driver finding above. P3-Q4 and P1-Q5 share findings are still met. A territory card with no `directions` still meets TRx findings as before.
- (b) The national card lists access, promotion or supply in `drivers_ruled_out` when every reportable territory (`p.ok`) rules it out on the brief's weeks: `lag_checks.national_ruled_out` runs the same `run_checks` the territory stories use, on a `Registry.scratch()` (same read-only connection, its own empty claims and evidence), so no claim or number is added. A territory whose check is supported or inconclusive keeps the driver off the list. `drivers_checked` then names competitor plus those drivers. Cost: about 5 checks per reportable territory, a few seconds a brief; not too slow, so no approximation was needed. On the anchors: P3-Q1 and P3-Q4 rule out access and supply; P1-Q5 rules out access and promotion (supply is live in Pacific, so it is correctly off the list).
- P3-Q1 still expects promotion ruled out as well; at least one territory's promotion check there is not ruled out, so the national card cannot claim it. Left as is (no approximation). P3-Q1's `bxa_share` pre-launch direction depends on the backend packet's `pre_launch`; this card still compares with the brief's comparison weeks until it lands.
